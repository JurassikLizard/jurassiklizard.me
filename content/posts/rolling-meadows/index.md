## {{< style class="rune" >}}abte{{< /style >}}

The rolling meadows were punctured by jagged breaks of crumbling rocks, connected by small stretches of scraggly woods. As the sun drifted down below the grass and ignited the shimmering air with a soft warm glow, A* took shelter in the nook of a rock formation. Sitting just on the edge of the woods and meadows, he settled his rucksack on the ground, and took stock of his provisions. After ensuring this evening's ration would still allow him to make it to H& with food to spare, he scraped together a tiny fire, heating up some salted cod and dried bread. Letting the flickering light warm up his face, he dozed off; propping up his head with his forearm.

The night air rolled through the hills, flowing for miles across the land before being funneled through the river valley K&, swirling and blasting out into the open sea. The old fisherman B*'s shack stood at the edge of this channel. When he was young he built it here for the winds and tides that advantaged going out to sea early morning, and coming in for the late afternoon. After ensuring his schooner was tied down for the upcoming storm, he hobbled up the dock to his hut and fell asleep to rhythmic creaks of the boards.

A* was not asleep for long before he startled awake by the pattering of hooves. The fire had gone out, yet the moon adequately illuminated the figure dismounting his saddle. He was of medium stature, with a scruffy beard. His horse carried a saddlebag, concealing an irregularly oblong object. The strangers' weapon was a blade, though the tip was fashioned in a curve that A* had not seen before, and neither did he recognize the patterns on the tunic and dark crimson patches on the strangers leather boots.
{{< softbreak >}}
"Are you a party of one?" drawled the man.
{{< softbreak >}}
"No, my companions are riding ahead on a hunt. They should be back by dawn." Though he had no partners and the last living thing he saw was a measly squirrel, it did not feel wise for A* to reveal his singularity.
{{< softbreak >}}
"May I join you here till the morn?"
{{< softbreak >}}
"Ah, I was just to set off, I have wasted enough time here as it is." "How long have you been riding?"
{{< softbreak >}}
At this, the man mumbled something which A* could not make out. "Where are you and your party heading?" continued the stranger, "I would like to make haste to the nearest town."

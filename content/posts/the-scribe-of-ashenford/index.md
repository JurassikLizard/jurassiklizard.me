## From the Ashenford Chronicle

In the winter of the king's ninth year, a rider came to Ashenford with a letter sealed in black wax. The old scribe broke the seal before the hearth and found, beneath the royal hand, a second message written in ink that had not yet dried.

He read it once, then folded the page and locked it away. By morning, the rider had vanished from the stables, and every bell in the keep was ringing.
